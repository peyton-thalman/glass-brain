Each tile is an advisor's book; its color is the share of their households below 75%. A neural network trained on the planning engine re-scores every household as the market moves. Hover a tile for the advisor's numbers.

All 100,000 households are made up, generated in your browser from a fixed seed. Market moves are real: S&P 500 daily closes and the 10-year Treasury yield from FRED.
