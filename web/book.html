Can you trust it? How the brain is checked against the engine
The households are made up. Replays use real S&P 500 daily closes and 10-year Treasury yields (FRED series SP500 and DGS10): the stock share of each portfolio moves with the index, the bond share with the yield, and cash is held flat. Dragging the slider moves stocks only. Every red flag can be checked against the real engine on its card. This is education and decision support, not personal advice.
