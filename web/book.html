S&P 500 since the plans were made

4,000 households, one cell each

The whole book, re-scored every frame

Each cell is a household, in the same place the whole time, sorted by how safe its plan was on the day it was made. A neural network trained on the planning engine re-scores all 4,000 under seven scenarios every time the market moves. Click any cell.

The households are made up. Market moves come from real S&P 500 daily closes (FRED series SP500). Only the stock share of each portfolio moves with the index; bonds and cash are held flat. Every red flag can be checked against the real engine on its card. This is education and decision support, not personal advice.
