What you're looking at

The front pages use a small "brain" (a neural network) so they can re-check thousands of families in a blink. This page lets you look inside it, the way you'd take the back off a watch.

It practiced

It studied 102 million made up retirements, good markets and bad, long lives and short, and learned which plans tend to last.

You can watch it think

Each dot is a neuron. Change a family's numbers and see which ones light up, and what each one cares about.

You can test it

Switch neurons off and see whether it still gets the right answer. That's how you know it learned real ideas, not tricks.

It learned the tax code

A second brain learned 2026 tax law from 3 million returns. Its bends line up with the real brackets, within a few dollars.

It's always checked

Every answer can be compared live with the full planning engine, so you can see exactly how close it is.

Why bother?

Speed. The engine takes a moment per family. The brain takes microseconds, fast enough to watch a whole firm on every market move.

Educational model. Real returns are drawn from a simple fat-ish normal market model (stocks 6.5% ± 17%, bonds 1.8% ± 6.5%, after inflation) and Gompertz mortality. It is not financial advice and not any firm's planning engine.
